Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you collect and deliver?

Yes. Collection and delivery is free on loads of 10kg and above. If your load is under 10kg it is R18. Message us on WhatsApp with your address and we'll confirm a slot.

How much does laundry cost?

Wash, dry & fold is R35 per kilogram, wash, dry, fold & iron is R70 per kilogram, and ironing only is R40 per kilogram. The minimum order is R150. Duvets start at R80 and blankets at R120, priced per item by size, and towels from R25. We confirm the full price with you before we start.

What is the booking deposit?

A R50 booking deposit secures your collection slot. This amount is taken off your final bill — it is not an extra fee, so you only pay what your laundry actually costs. Ask us about it on WhatsApp.

What time can I drop off and collect?

We collect and drop off between 06:30–10:00 in the morning and 17:00–20:00 in the evening, every day. Book your slot on WhatsApp and we will confirm it.

How long does it take?

Standard turnaround is 24 to 48 hours. Message us in the morning slot and we will aim to have your laundry back the same evening where the load allows.

Do I need to weigh my laundry?

No. Our driver weighs your bag at collection, itemises it with you, and you approve the price before anything is washed. You only pay for what you actually hand over.

Are there extra charges for hard stains?

Standard pricing covers normal washing. Special treatment may be quoted separately for heavily soiled items, excessive pet hair, oil or grease, and difficult stains. We always tell you the extra before we start, so there are no surprises on the final bill.

What should I do with my laundry?

Put it in any bag and leave a note of anything that needs special attention - a stubborn stain, an item you would rather not tumble dry, or a shirt you want extra sharp. If you are unsure, WhatsApp us a photo first.

Which areas do you service?

We cover Pretoria and the surrounding suburbs including Pretoria, Centurion, Menlyn, Brooklyn, Soshanguve. If your area is not listed, message us - we are often able to add new collection routes.

How do I pay?

We accept cash, EFT and card payment. For regular weekly collections we can set up a monthly invoice. Ask us on WhatsApp for the details.
